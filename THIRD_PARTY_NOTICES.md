## Garden illustrations

The garden and character images in `public/garden/` were generated with the built-in image generation tool for the user-requested Plants vs. Zombies themed design. They were not extracted from a game installation or copied from the reference screenshots. Prompts and asset processing are recorded in [the art notes](docs/art-prompts-pvz.md). The car frame, article shelves, icons and interactions are implemented in project code.
